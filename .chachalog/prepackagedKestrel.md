A new pre-packaged site, Kestrel Bay Wealth (`kestrel-bay-prepackaged-website`): a fictional Hong Kong wealth manager in English, Traditional Chinese and Simplified Chinese, built with the template set alone, with home page blocks that differ by language, three site groups with rights by section (editors of one section, a reviewer who approves publications) and an article left unpublished for an authoring demonstration. `scripts/export-prepackaged.py --site kestrel-bay` writes it: users are left out and a section personalised with jExperience is packaged as its default version.
