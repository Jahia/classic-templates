Formidable forms follow the site theme in light and dark, live and in Page Builder. A colour field shows a swatch instead of a thin line across the column; the file button takes the site's colours; fields inside a step or a fieldset are spaced like the others, and a fieldset's title reads as a group heading; the step numbers are readable in dark mode; a consent box sits beside its sentence again. In Page Builder, Formidable's actions zones, field actions and the fields a rule hides use the site's surfaces instead of light grey cards, and a field a rule hides keeps its full width.
